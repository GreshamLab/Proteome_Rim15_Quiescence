# Published supplementary datasets (local only, not tracked)

Supplement files for the cross-dataset phosphosite comparison
(`revision3/R02_phosphosite_overlap.Rmd`). Kept out of git (~120 MB,
publisher-distributed files); copies live in the lab Google Drive under
`Sun et al.,/PLoS Genetics 3rd Submission/Related proteome studies/`.

| Folder | Study | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `baro2018/` | Baro et al. 2018 (PP2A-Cdc55 phosphoproteome) | Additional files 1–14 |
| `dokladal2021/` | Dokladal et al. 2021 (TORC1/Rim15) | mmc1–mmc5 |
| `li2019/` | Li et al. 2019 (Rim15-dependent phosphoproteome) | mmc1–mmc8 |
| `plank2020/` | Plank et al. 2020 (quiescence phosphoproteome) | supplement |

Derived harmonized site tables are committed to `revision3/output/`.
